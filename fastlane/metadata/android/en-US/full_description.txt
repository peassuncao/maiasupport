MaiaSupport exposes local Maia-3 move inference to other Android chess apps through a documented AIDL bound service. On first launch choose Maia-3 5M (21 MB, lower storage and memory use) or Maia-3 79M (316 MB, larger and may provide more accurate predictions). The size is shown before download; each download requires explicit confirmation and is checked by size and SHA-256. A compatible model can also be imported manually. Clients provide FEN, Elo values and legal moves; MaiaSupport returns the highest-scoring legal move asynchronously. No model is bundled in the APK. No ads, analytics or Google Play Services. Independent community software, not affiliated with the University of Toronto CSSLab or Maia Chess.
